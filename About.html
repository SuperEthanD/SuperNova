About SuperNova

A lightweight, space-themed web browser and search portal crafted directly on iOS.

Our Mission

SuperNova is designed to make exploring the web feel fast, focused, and cosmic. Combine live knowledge lookups with your own custom-indexed websites in one clean interface.

How Search Works

When you search on SuperNova, it first scans your locally indexed websites submitted through the Developer Portal, then queries live Wikipedia articles to bring back instant answers.
